# About Me

I am a recently graduated senior from Yale University, where I majored in Physics (Intensive) and Computer Science. I am broadly interested in exoplanets, with a focus on exoplanet dynamics and substellar atmospheres. I have researched double hot Jupiter formation through von Zeipel–Lidov–Kozai migration in binary star systems and the atmospheres of directly imaged gas giants and brown dwarfs though atmospheric retrievals. I am currently working secular chaos induced high-eccentricity tidal migration in "3+1" quadruple systems. See more in the [research](https://yurouninaliu.github.io/research/) section.

Beyond astrophysics research, I've designed my computer science major around computer graphics and game engines. I'm interested in coding creative projects that bridge science and storytelling.

I will begin my graduate studies in astrophysics at Princeton University in Fall 2026.
