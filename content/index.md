# About Me

I am a senior at Yale University studying Physics (Intensive) and Computer Science, with a focus on exoplanetary science. I am researching exoplanet dynamics and substellar atmospheres. I have researched double hot Jupiter formation through von Zeipel–Lidov–Kozai migration in binary star systems and the atmospheres of directly imaged gas giants and brown dwarfs though atmospheric retrievals. I am currently working secular chaos induced high-eccentricity tidal migration in "3+1" quadruple systems. See more in the [research](https://yurouninaliu.github.io/research/) section.

Beyond astrophysics research, I've designed my computer science major around computer graphics and game engines. I'm interested in coding creative projects that bridge science and storytelling.
